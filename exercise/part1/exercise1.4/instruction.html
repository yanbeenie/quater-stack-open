Safe use of a gas cylinder

These instructions are for anyone cooking with a gas cylinder at home follow them before lightining the gas and whenever you change the cylinder

What you need

  • A gas cylinder in good conditino
  • A suitable gas regulatorand hose
  • A stable cooking stove
  • soapy water for checking connection

    • Steps

    1. Place the gas cylinder upright in a well-ventiledarea,away from flames and heat.
    2. Check the hose,regulator,and connection for damage before use
    3. Apply soapy water to the connection and look for bubbles tocheck for leaks. Never use a flameto test for leaks.
    4. IF there are no leaks,follow the sove manufacture instructions to light the burner safely
